# Aevum sound effects - sources and licences

All files are real recordings (no synthesized or AI-generated audio), converted with ffmpeg to mono 44.1 kHz MP3, trimmed and peak-normalized.

- **LIB** = the free "1000+ UR.FASTEDITOR SFX" pack (no explicit redistribution licence; these files are removed on the rights holder's request)
- **KIT** = owner's normalized kit `D:/Elara/06-araclar/sfx/kit/` (derived from the same library)
- **KENNEY** = Kenney "RPG Audio" pack (kenney.nl), Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
- **KIMPACT** = Kenney "Impact Sounds" 1.0 (https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), CC0 1.0 (License.txt in the pack)
- **SNM** = Still North Media "Medieval Sound Effects: Weapon Impacts", part 1 (https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-sound-effects-weapon-impacts), CC0 ("Our team holds CC0 NO RIGHTS RESERVED"); real recorded weapon-on-weapon hits
- **BAS** = "Battle at Sea" (https://opengameart.org/content/battle-at-sea), CC0
- **ADB** = artisticdude "Battle Sound Effects" (https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects), released under CC0 (also GPL/CC-BY; CC0 used)
- **ADS** = artisticdude "Swishes Sound Pack" (https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack), CC0
- **JSW** = Jan Schupke "Fantasy Weapons and Apparel SFX Library" (https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-weapons-and-apparel-sfx-library), CC0 (readme.txt in the pack; "organic and unprocessed")
- **RD100** = rubberduck "100 CC0 SFX" (https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx), CC0
- **RD100v2** = rubberduck "100 CC0 SFX #2" (https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2), CC0
- **RD80** = rubberduck "80 CC0 RPG SFX" (https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx), CC0
- **GRUNTS** = "Grunts - male death and pain" (https://opengameart.org/content/grunts-male-death-and-pain), CC0 (voice recordings)
- **YELL** = "Male grunt/yelling sounds" (https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds), dual CC0 / OGA-BY 3.0; used under CC0

Combat files are cut from the source recordings (onset-trimmed, 5 ms fade-in, cosine tail fade), some are layers of two or three real recordings mixed together ("+"), resampled ("@rate") where noted, loudness-normalised per category, mono 44.1 kHz MP3 112 kbps. Nothing synthesized or AI-generated.
